    02 / Make your ownOne sentence in. A living mascot out.

    Describe it.
    Get it.

    Hand your coding agent the Wobnimo skill and one sentence. It designs the character as data, measures every part against the engine's own geometry, renders a contact sheet, and fixes what looks off until it's right. No 3D tools. No rigging. No animation.

    10 / 10 mascots below came from one sentence each

    your agent

    made
    live!

    An animation replays an agent building each mascot from its sentence: plain shapes first, then a face, then colour, then a passing check, then the mascot comes alive.

    1. 1 / Give your agent the skill

      Works with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode and any agent that reads skills.

    2. 2 / Describe your mascot

      Paste it into your agent. It installs Wobnimo, builds the mascot, checks it and wires it to your app.

    3. 3 / Watch it check itself

      $ npx wobnimo check✖ eye buried 0.028 inside the headfix: move it to z 0.262✓ 0 errors · 0 warnings

      Every message comes with the exact number to change, so even small models get it right.
